How we calculate ✳

A footprint estimate should be easy to understand. Here’s what goes into each number.

✳
These are illustrative demo factors.

They help show how the app works; they are not a certified inventory or a claim about your actual emissions. Replace them with current, location-specific factors before making business decisions.

↗COMMUTING

Distance × days × mode

We use the one-way distance and multiply it by two for a return journey, then by the number of commute days.

Car0.192 kg / km
Bus0.105 kg / km
Train0.041 kg / km
Bike or walking0 kg / km
✈WORK TRAVEL

Trip distance × mode

Enter the total journey distance for the work trip. For flights, use the total distance across all legs.

Car0.192 kg / km
Train0.035 kg / km
Flight0.255 kg / km
⌂REMOTE ENERGY

Energy × days × grid factor

Estimate extra home energy used while working remotely. We multiply this by an illustrative electricity factor.

Home energy1.5 kWh / day
Electricity0.207 kg / kWh
Example: 10 days3.1 kg CO2e
ⓘ

Not a formal carbon inventory. These simple estimates exclude many lifecycle effects and other sources of workplace emissions. Factors are fixed illustrative values in this prototype. A production version should cite trusted, current sources, support regional factors, and keep a record of factor versions.
